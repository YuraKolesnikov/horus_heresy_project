## Notes

Legionary Dror was a World Eaters veteran of the 8th Assault Company, betrayed and nearly killed at Isstvan III. Recovered alive but mentally shattered and unable to distinguish friend from foe, he was transported to Bodt alongside hundreds of similarly wounded Legionaries and inducted into the Red Butchers. During Autek Mor's invasion of Tredecimmia, Dror reportedly killed at least twelve Loyalist Astartes and twenty-four Solar Auxilia before being crushed beneath an Iron Hands Mastodon.

Like other Red Butchers, Dror wore Terminator armour that could be remotely locked between battles to restrain his uncontrollable violence. His original blue Legion heraldry had increasingly been replaced by red markings and the blood of his enemies.
