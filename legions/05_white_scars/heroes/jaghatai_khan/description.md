### Chogoris and the Great Khan

3. Jaghatai Khan: Conquest and Freedom on Chogoris

The legends of Jaghatai’s youth are strongly embellished, but their consistent core places him among the horse-riding tribes of Chogoris’s Empty Quarter. Adopted into a nomadic clan, he became a formidable rider, hunter and leader. A rival tribe, identified in some accounts as the Kurayed, murdered his adoptive father and his companions. Jaghatai avenged them and then broke the recurring cycle of tribal vendetta: defeated enemies could join his growing people rather than perpetuate endless retaliatory war.

His talent for uniting rival tribes developed into a campaign that overthrew the settled Palatine civilisation dominating much of Chogoris. Through speed, surprise, alliances and relentless campaigning, he became the Khan of Khans and brought the planet under his rule. When the Emperor eventually found him, Jaghatai had already won his world. He acknowledged the Emperor as father and accepted a place in the wider Imperial endeavour, but was never naturally inclined to treat sovereign obedience as the highest virtue. His bond was personal and founded upon respect rather than surrender of judgement.

Chogoris—also called Mundus Planus—became the Legion’s primary home world. The culture of its nomads, their horsemanship, oaths, poetry, feuds and ritual practices would become the basis of a new identity for a Legion previously scattered across the stars. Yet Jaghatai’s loyalty to the wider Imperium remained inseparable from his conviction that he and his warriors must retain the freedom to choose their own course.

The Khan's Unanswered Questions

Jaghatai Khan emerged from Chondax with a similarly bitter victory. His Legion had survived the Alpha Legion's trap, but the conflict had exposed the unreliability of former allies and the dangerous uncertainty surrounding the rebellion.

The Khan's search for answers led him toward Prospero, where the fate of Magnus and the Thousand Sons would further complicate his understanding of loyalty and betrayal. Rather than immediately surrendering his judgement to any faction, Jaghatai sought to discover the truth independently, determined that his Legion would choose its future on its own terms.
