## Notes

### Karaoghlanlar

Karaoghlanlar · “Dark Sons of Death”. Black-clad Destroyer formations employing proscribed weaponry for total annihilation and certain post-campaign rituals. Answered to the Council of Seers rather than a single Brotherhood; used only by agreement of the Great Khan and Stormseers.
