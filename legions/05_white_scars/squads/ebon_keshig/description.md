### Ebon Keshig

Ebon Keshig

The Kharash served as a ritual post of atonement and redemption for sins and breaches of honour within the White Scars, and a quick path to glory. Deployed in the van of sieges and other high-intensity conflicts, the Kharash served to shield the more fragile contingents of the White Scars, atoning for their perceived sins not only through the pursuit of martial glory, but through the protection of their brothers. Clad in black ceramite, the Ebon Keshig were instantly recognisable both as formidable warriors to the enemy, and to their brothers. Those who survived their service within the Ebon Keshig were deemed to have proven themselves worthy in the eyes of the Legion, often returning to either their previous posts or sometimes to other roles fitting their new mien.

Tactical Dreadnought Armour typically found little favour among the White Scars. Yet as with all conflicts, during the Great Crusade each Legion found themselves utilising everything they had at their disposal to fight in the name of, or against, the fledgling Imperium. Among the White Scars, the Kharash was one of the only units to utilise Tactical Dreadnought Armour, as the Ebon Keshig relied less upon speed and agility than they did on sheer strength and size.

Legionary Surentai — Ebon Keshig of the White Scars

Legionary Surentai originally served as a line infantryman in the Brotherhood of the Sable Wolf. Although an experienced and capable warrior, he had earned little distinction before the assault on Black-blight, when he volunteered to join the Ebon Keshig at the Great Khan's call.

The Ebon Keshig were a specialised formation of the White Scars equipped with rare, black-painted Terminator armour, employed for particularly dangerous assaults and desperate last stands. Unlike conventional Terminator formations, their armour was generally issued temporarily for a single battle and returned to the Legion's vaults afterward. Individual deployments were known as Kharash, and many suits possessed long histories of sacrifice and extraordinary bravery.

Surentai received the Cataphractii-pattern armour Monqara Erden, a Chogorian name roughly translated as “Eternal Dark Jewel.” During the assault on Black-blight, he killed numerous enemies but suffered grievous wounds.

Personally commended by Jaghatai Khan for his courage, Surentai survived and returned to the Brotherhood of the Sable Wolf with the rank of Kavkhan, his service in the Ebon Keshig having transformed an otherwise unremarkable Legionary into an honoured veteran.
