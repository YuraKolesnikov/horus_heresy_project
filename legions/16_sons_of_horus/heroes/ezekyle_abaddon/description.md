## Notes

In the Manachean War, First Captain Abaddon commanded the Sons of Horus assault on Hive Ilium from the battle barge *King Eater*. After eleven days of fighting he personally led the final assault on Manachea's Palace of Light and killed Imperial Commander Pryamus Beket.
