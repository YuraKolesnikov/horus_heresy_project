## Notes
Some Atramentar adorn their armour or shoulder-plates with metalic representations of sabre-toothed Nostraman lions. These crag cougars were native to the world's mountainous, rural areas and sometimes hunted by the wealthy of Nostramo's cities as a sign of status.
