## Notes

Horus manipulated the confrontation in which Leman Russ and the Space Wolves attacked Magnus and the Thousand Sons on Prospero. After significant casualties Russ withdrew to Alaxxes and later established contact with Jaghatai Khan.

### Dulan

The Barbarian and the Knight — Dulan and the Space Wolves

The battle at Dulan is usually remembered for the Lion and Leman Russ' legendary duel after the Dark Angels seized the final victory while the Space Wolves were still besieging the world. The source challenges the popular assumption that the incident created an enduring hatred between the two Legions. It notes numerous campaigns before and after Dulan in which they fought together, often with shared camaraderie. Both Primarchs valued directness, loyalty and deeds over ceremony, and both treated oaths as binding. Their cooperation during the Rangdan wars and their reunion towards the end of the Heresy are stronger evidence of their relationship than later tales of an unending feud. The duel is accepted as historical; the enduring rancour often attributed to it is treated as doubtful. Dulan is therefore interpreted as a test of two proud warriors rather than the origin of irreconcilable hostility.
