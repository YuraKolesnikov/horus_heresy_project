# Space Marine Legion Wargear

Great Crusade & early Horus Heresy • Weapons, ammunition and equipment

## I. Ranged Weapons

Archaeotech Pistol — Rare sidearms surviving from Old Night, prized for exceptional firepower and craftsmanship. Designs range from micro-atomic projectile weapons to exotic energy pistols drawing power from a planetary magnetosphere. Usually entrusted to Imperial agents and senior Legion warriors.

Combi-bolter — Two standard bolters coupled to fire together. Developed for the Terminator armour project, this bulky but effective prototype became increasingly widespread by the Heresy.

Conversion Beam Weapons — Ancient energy weapons that induce subatomic implosion, increasing in power with distance until the beam becomes unstable. Their complexity, calibration and reactor demands restrict them chiefly to siege and starship-breaching specialists such as Techmarines and Mechanicum Destructors. Heavy versions can equip suitably powered vehicles, including Contemptor Dreadnoughts.

Graviton Weapons — Poorly understood relics that manipulate gravity to incapacitate enemies and damage machinery. Especially useful aboard starships or in zero gravity, where avoiding secondary explosions is valuable. Powerful settings can injure even armoured targets. Graviton guns and cannons are variants of this family.

Grenade Harness — An auxiliary fragmentation weapon mounted on Terminator armour, intended to disrupt enemies as the wearer charges into close combat.

Needle Pistol — A silent, sophisticated assassin’s sidearm favoured by the Terran Courts before Unification. The finest examples combine a subtle laser strike with a venomous projectile, allowing them to threaten exceptionally resilient targets.

Phosphex Bombs & Medusa Shells — A rare, corrosive incendiary substance that produces a white-green, movement-seeking flame in contact with air. It attacks flesh and metal, is notoriously difficult to extinguish and leaves severe contamination. Issued as bombs and heavy artillery shells, phosphex remained a weapon of last resort.

Plasma Weaponry (Crusade Era) — The early Imperium possessed more sophisticated plasma technology than later ages, although it remained hazardous. Rare variants included the Plasma Blaster, Phased-Plasma Fusil and Executioner Cannon, all destined to become virtually unknown in later millennia.

Rad Grenades & Rad Missiles — Specialised radiological weapons, normally reserved for dangerous xenoforms. Their radiation and contamination weaken survivors as well as killing outright. Rad missiles originated in the gene-war anarchy of Old Night.

## I. Ranged Weapons (continued)

Breacher Charge — A powerful, cumbersome siege explosive used at close quarters to destroy armoured emplacements, fortifications and barricades.

Reaper Autocannon — A compact, rapid-firing support weapon developed for Tactical Dreadnought Armour. Its substantial recoil requires the stabilising mass and strength of a Terminator suit.

Rotor Cannon — A motor-driven, multi-barrelled stub weapon favoured by the Imperial Army and adopted by some Legions. Its projectiles are less powerful than bolts, but its immense volume of fire suits lightly armoured hordes and fleshy xenoforms. The prototype assault cannon represents a more powerful development of this principle.

Shroud Bombs — Canisters that generate dense grey smoke and electromagnetic interference, concealing troops from both visual observation and electronic sensors.

Volkite Weapons — Powerful heat-ray weapons dating to the Age of Strife. They burn organic matter with violent secondary effects and deliver exceptional lethality for their size. Difficult production and the Crusade’s growing demands led to their replacement by the more practical Terran bolter; by the Heresy, volkites were largely confined to specialised units. Variants include the Serpenta, Charger, Caliver and Culverin.

## II. Special-Issue Bolter Munitions

Tempest Bolt Shells — Fragmentation ammunition that showers targets with shrapnel at the expense of effective range.

Kraken Bolt Shells — Enhanced adamantine-core ammunition with superior penetration and range, used by Seeker squads. Its production cost prevents general issue.

Scorpius Bolt Shells — Rare, individually crafted munitions employing a guided penetrator with a destructive thermal effect against armour. Temperamental and manually loaded, they are produced by Legion Techmarines.

## III. Close Combat Weapons

Chainaxe — Originating with the World Eaters and associated with Angron, the chainaxe uses a heavier blade and more powerful motor than a chainsword. It is difficult to wield but effective against armour, and notorious for its brutality.

Charnabal Sabre — An elegant weapon descended from Terran duelling societies and assassin cults of the Age of Strife. Its intricate forged blade rewards speed and dexterity rather than brute force. Favoured by some Legion officers over power weapons, while others regard it as decadent.

Combat Blades — A broad category of melee weapons reflecting each Legion’s traditions, ranging from chain-bayonets to adamantine gladii and heavy Thule-alloy battle-axes.

Deathshroud Power Scythe — Also called a man-reaper, this heavy power weapon belongs to the Death Guard’s specialised martial tradition. Its sweeping reach and the Legion’s training allow a warrior to engage multiple foes at once.

Digital Lasers — Miniaturised xenos weapons concealed in rings, gauntlets, sword hilts or helmets. Their extremely short range makes them especially suited to surprise strikes during close combat.

Lascutter — An unwieldy industrial cutting tool designed for armoured bulkheads and stubborn ores. Its concentrated laser arc makes it a potent improvised weapon in siege warfare.

Paragon Blade — A rare category of exceptionally lethal mastercrafted weapons or ancient relics, often finely balanced two-handed power weapons, but sometimes exotic chain weapons or mysterious sickle swords from death worlds near the Halo Stars.

Servo-arm — A heavy industrial manipulator often linked directly to the nervous system of a Techmarine or Magos Adept. Primarily a repair and maintenance tool, it can also deliver crushing blows in melee.

## IV. Armour & Battlefield Equipment

Artificer Armour — Superior power armour fashioned by Mechanicum master craftsmen. Often decorated with intricate markings, it may incorporate archaic designs and exceptional protective technologies.

Augury Scanner — A sensor used to detect hidden or approaching enemies, including the electro-gravitic disturbances that precede teleportation.

Cameleoline — A refractive material that blends with surrounding colours. Woven into cloaks or applied to armour, it aids concealment and covert operations.

Cognis-signum — An integrated array of sensors, cogitators, communications and telemetry that allows a warrior to process and coordinate battlefield information with remarkable speed.

Combat Shields & Boarding Shields — Personal defensive field generators housed in wrist-mounted bucklers or larger shields. Boarding shields are especially suited to Breacher squads fighting in confined starship corridors or sieges; both offer less protection than the emerging prototype storm shield.

Legion Vexilla & Standard — Battlefield banners and icons that embody the identity, traditions and achievements of squads, Companies and Chapters. They act as rallying points and symbols of Legion honour.

Narthecium — Specialised medical and alchemical equipment carried by Legion Apothecaries to sustain wounded battle-brothers during combat.

Nuncio-vox — A communications and targeting beacon usually integrated into a modified power armour backpack. It links dispersed forces with nearby troops and orbiting vessels, assisting reinforcement and fire-support coordination.

Power Armour — The signature armour of the Legiones Astartes, combining composite plating, an electro-fibre musculature, internal power systems, sensors and environmental seals. Descended from Unification-era technology and refined with Mechanicum expertise, it included the widespread Crusade and Maximus patterns; Iron armour was particularly favoured for siege and close assault.

Refractor Fields & Iron Halos — Rare personal field generators that deflect physical and energy impacts. Their power demands and mobility requirements limit their protection. Usually incorporated into armour or amulets and awarded to officers and honoured champions.

Cyber-familiar — A semi-autonomous servant such as a servo-skull, mek-spider or lesser haemonculite, linked to its operator’s neural control. It extends the senses and capabilities of Techmarines and Mechanicum adepts.

Terminator Armour (Tactical Dreadnought Armour) — Developed for heavy assault and space-hulk combat, drawing partly on the shielded industrial suits of Mechanicum Solar Adepts. Its powerful exoskeleton and internal energy supply provide remarkable protection at the cost of bulk. Indomitus, Tartaros and Saturnine patterns were developed during the late Great Crusade and were largely comparable in function.

Cataphractii Terminator Armour — An early, exceptionally well-protected pattern with slab-like ceramite shoulder plates housing additional shield generators. The strain on its exoskeleton reduces mobility, contributing to its decline in some Legions by the Heresy.

## V. Mobility & Heavy Weapon Support

Legion Space Marine Bike — Used for reconnaissance, pursuit and shock assaults. Patterns include the ancient Iron Shadow and the Wyvern, originally developed on distant human worlds and strengthened and armoured for Astartes use.

Legion Space Marine Jetbike — Rare, fast combat machines using compact grav-repeller motors manufactured at only a handful of Imperial sources.

Suspensor Web — A sophisticated device that partially offsets the weight of a portable heavy weapon, allowing more mobile employment at the expense of effective range.
