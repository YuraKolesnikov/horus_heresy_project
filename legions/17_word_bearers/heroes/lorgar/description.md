# Lorgar Aurelian

**Rank:** Primarch  
**Title:** Bearer of the Word  

## Appearance
**Weapon:** Crozius Illuminarum  

## Notes
- Thousands of individual Colchisian glyphs gold-inked onto the primarch’s white flesh
- Weapon’s haft was the cream of ivory, reinforced by a grip of black iron. Its head was an orb of adamantium, stained black through a forgemaster’s touch and decorated with silver-leafed runes. Evenly-spaced spikes the length of human forearms projected from its outer edges, lending the mace a brutish air almost at odds with the philosophical seeker who carried it across the stars.
- He cast about for somewhere to sit, but found nothing equal to his weight and bulk, and so sat on the floor in front of Kor Phaeron's desk, his head still level with the standing archdeacon.
- Lorgar was the image of the Emperor in an unknowable younger life - an immortal at thirty.
- Lorgar was as magnificent and terrible to behold as ever. His scalp was completely hairless, and every inch of exposed flesh was caked in gold leaf scripture, so that he gleamed like a statue of living metal. The sockets of his soulful, impossibly intense eyes were blackened with kohl, and Sor Talgron was, as ever, unable to hold the Urizen’s gaze for more than a fraction of second.
