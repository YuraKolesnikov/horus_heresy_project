### Istvaan V
Raven Guard recon ship, Ad Temperesta, was the first to break the real-space at the edge of the system. Scanning and remaining unseen, it journeyed on a wide orbit of Istvaan’s star.  

The Raven Guard infiltrator’s reconnaissance had been thorough, identifying the vast majority of the Traitor’s activity planet-side as being confined to an area called Urgall Depression, with fresh bastions and defence lines raised by the Traitors, using the eroded alien battlements as their foundations.  

To the right flank was a maze of broken rock piles, jutting mesas and sudden deadfalls more than four kilometres wide which several companies of Sons of Horus picked for their own killing ground. Knowing full well their skill in manoeuvre and fluid warfare, Ferrus Manus’ plan had committed the bulk of the Raven Guard, and in this quarter the battle took on a life of its own, becoming a fractured anarchy of hit and run combats. Over the tangle of canyons and killing grounds, Corax and his assault companies swept in on jets of fire, decapitating and dismembering swathes of the enemy.. But the sons of the archtraitor fought on with bitter arrogance..  

After 3 hours of battle, Salamanders and Raven Guard began to fall back to regroup and rearm, having expended their supplies. It is held in some sources that, upon the arrival of four fresh Legions, Corax was the first to call for a withdrawal for those fighting in favour of the newcomers to take the lead. His Legion had suffered severely in the battle and he had no desire to waste further lives in pointless attrition.  

..The members of Raven Guard came within close enough distance of the Word Bearers to recognise and hail some of them by name before slaughter began. At point blank range the unsuspecting Legionaries of the Salamanders and the Raven Guard were cut down by the hundreds by those they thought were brothers.  

### Istvaan System Compliance
Brought Istvaan III to Compliance under Corax command.

The black-hulled strike cruisers and gunships of XIXth Legion attacked without warning from the planetary night, systemically destroying Istvaan III’s limited military infrastructure and taking charge of its seats of governance, turning senate into bombed-out ruin and those that had led their world to deny the Imperial Truth, were either dead or in chains.
