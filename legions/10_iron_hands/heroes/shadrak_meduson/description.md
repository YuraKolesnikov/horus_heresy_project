## Notes

After Ferrus Manus' death and the Iron Hands' fragmentation, Shadrak Meduson gradually brought scattered survivors into a loosely coordinated resistance movement.

After the remaining Iron Hands Clan-Council was destroyed at Oqueth Minor, Shadrak Meduson of Clan Sorrgol assumed command of the largest surviving formations. He united more than 20,000 Legionaries into independent, mixed-Legion strike cells and dispersed them throughout Traitor-controlled territory. For three years, these forces conducted thousands of raids, assassinations, and sabotage operations, gradually weakening Horus' armies and delaying their advance towards Terra. Their campaign was so carefully coordinated that even Horus initially failed to recognise that the seemingly unrelated attacks were directed by a single commander.

Having arrived at Isstvan V in time to bear witness to the Warmaster's betrayal, it was all Meduson could do to fight clear with as many Iron Hands, Salamanders and Raven Guard as he could rescue. Eight weeks later, the Legion's Clan-Council gathered at Oqueth Minor to take command of the Iron Hands Legion. But their vessel was ambushed and the Council slain by the hand of Tybalt Marr of the Sons of Horus Legion and his Emperor's Children allies.

It fell to Shadrak Meduson to muster the survivors and lead them in a new kind of war that incorporated the battle doctrines of all three Legions. His goal was twofold - exact bloody revenge and slow the Warmaster's advance on Terra. Alloying the disparate elements of the three Legions in his force, Meduson created a hidden network of attack cells that ranged far and wide, launching hit and run attacks against the Traitors while concealing their overall strategy until his most audacious attack to date - against Warmaster Horus himself at the world of Dwell.
